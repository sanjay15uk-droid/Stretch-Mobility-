GuideHow to get the most out of it

Stop and get checked urgently if

  • You get numbness around your groin, bum or inner thighs
  • Any change in bladder or bowel control
  • Your leg or foot is getting weaker, or you're catching your toes
  • Pain in both legs, or back pain with fever or feeling unwell

These need same-day medical attention (A&E or 111), not stretching.

The golden rule

Stretch to mild tension, never sharp pain. If a move sends pain further down your leg, skip it for today. Pain that moves back up towards your lower back is usually a good sign.

Your daily plan

  • Monday to Saturday: Back & sciatica relief, once a day
  • Sunday: Evening unwind, a lighter day
  • Any day: a desk break after long drives or an hour or more sat down

Missed a day? Just carry on. Don't double up. Morning wake-up is there as a swap if you're stiff first thing.

Nerve glides

These are gentle "flossing" moves, not stretches. Small, smooth, no holding. A little tingle that fades straight away is fine. If it lingers, make the movement smaller next time.

Keep the screen on

The app keeps your screen awake during a session and beeps on changes. If you can't hear beeps, check your phone isn't on silent.

Backup

Your history lives on this phone only. Copy a backup to Notes or iCloud now and then.

Not a substitute for a physio. If things aren't improving after a few weeks, get it assessed.
